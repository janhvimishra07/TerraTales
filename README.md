# 🌍 Terra Tales – Tracking Forest Change Through NASA Data  
🚀 NASA Space Apps Challenge – Global Nominee  

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Terra Tales is an interactive environmental storytelling platform that brings the story of deforestation and forest fires to life using authentic NASA satellite data.

The project leverages MODIS (Moderate Resolution Imaging Spectroradiometer) data from NASA’s Terra Satellite to visualize forest cover changes, deforestation trends, and fire-affected regions through dynamic Power BI dashboards and embedded NASA visual resources.

By combining data visualization, storytelling, and education, Terra Tales transforms complex satellite data into an accessible, engaging, and emotionally impactful experience.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Deforestation and forest fires are accelerating globally, yet large-scale satellite data often remains inaccessible or difficult for the general public to interpret.

Terra Tales addresses this gap by:

- Converting raw NASA satellite data into intuitive dashboards
- Enabling multi-scale forest analysis (local to global)
- Making environmental data accessible for all age groups
- Promoting sustainability awareness through storytelling

- Content drafting and grammar refinement

No AI-generated NASA logos, mission identifiers, or satellite images were used.
